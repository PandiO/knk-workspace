# Phase sub-issue template

On approval the design session files one sub-issue per phase under the feature's issue (`knk-design` § 6.3), with
`issue_write` (`method: create`, `parent_issue_number: <feature issue>`). Labels: the type (`feature` or
`improvement`) and exactly one `tier:<n>`. The coordinator scopes one link by it, so it must stand on its own.

Title: `<Feature>: phase <p>, <phase name>`. Copy the body from the line below and replace every `<...>`.

---

```markdown
Phase <p> of [<feature> design](https://github.com/PandiO/knk-workspace/blob/next/docs/specs/<feature>/DESIGN.md)
§ 11, Decided <date>. Parent: PandiO/knk-workspace#<feature issue>.

1. **Scope:** <what this phase builds, in two or three sentences>.
2. **Binding:** DESIGN § <sections>, decisions <ids>.
3. **Acceptance:** <A-ids>, each a test in the right suite (`knk-test-writer`) or a smoke step.
4. **Repos:** <knk-web-api / knk-web-app / knk-plugin / knk-workspace>.
5. **Flag:** <`config.yml` key or setting, off by default / none>.
6. **Migration:** <additive migration allowed: what / none>.
7. **Depends on:** <phase or issue, or "none">.
8. **Smoke test:** [guide § <n>](https://github.com/PandiO/knk-workspace/blob/next/docs/guides/<feature>-smoke-test.md)
   (draft from the design; <none for tier:1 / short / full>).
9. **Tier:** tier:<n>, because <reason>.

---

```
