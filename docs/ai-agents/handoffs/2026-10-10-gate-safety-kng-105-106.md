## What changed

**KNG-105 (`b95b1c6`)**
- `knk-core/.../gates/safety/GateSafeSpotFinder`: the shared standing-spot search. Builds on teleport's
  `SafeLocationFinder.isSafe` (feet + head passable, solid ground, no lava/fire/cactus..., height range) and adds a
  cell filter (feet, head and ground must not be blocked) and a score: horizontal distance + 0.75/block height
  difference + 2 for "beside the door" + 4 for "the side opposite the preferred one". Deterministic ties.
- `knk-paper/.../gates/GateSafeSpots.nextToDoor`: within 4 blocks of the door's region (`GateDoorBounds`: closed
  footprint + captured regions, so an open door's opening is excluded too), not in any current gate block
  (`GateSpatialIndex`) or another door's region within 48 blocks; faces the door.
- `/gatedoor tp`: that spot, or "No safe spot to stand within 4 blocks of door '…'; not teleporting."
- `/gate tp`: the structure's spawn point (`DomainLocationResolver.structureLocation`, API-then-cache, 5 s timeout)
  first; else the first door's safe spot, and the reply says why (no spawn point set / lookup failed / spawn world
  not loaded). Works for a gate without loaded doors when it has a spawn point.
- Wiring: `KnkAdminCommand.setGateSpawnLookup` (one setter, **outside this row's claim**: KNG-107 owns that file;
  kept to one self-contained method next to `setGateTargetingSettings`), two lines + one helper in `KnKPlugin`.

**KNG-106 (`f157372`)**
- `knk-core/.../gates/safety/GateSweep`: the cells a door's blocks occupy or cross between two frames, every frame
  in between, plus the box between consecutive positions (covers a drawbridge's arc even at > 1 cell per frame),
  closed-scan and open-only blocks. Closing counts frames down.
- `CollisionPredictor` rewritten: `upcomingCells` = previous update .. two updates ahead, plus the resting frame's
  cells (rasterized gap-fill included) when the window reaches it. **Bug fixed:** it used to step frames upwards
  only, so a closing door was checked against frames it had already left.
- `GateAnimationTask.handleEntityPush`: entities overlapping the upcoming cells (players, mobs, items, vehicles with
  their riders; not spectators, displays, hanging entities) are moved **before** the blocks are placed by
  `EntityEvacuator` to `GateSafeSpotFinder`'s spot within 6 blocks, own side first, outside the door's remaining
  sweep, current gate blocks and other doors' regions. Fallback: the velocity push.
- `EntityPusher`: **bug fixed** — it always pushed along the face direction, i.e. pushed anyone behind the door
  through it; it now pushes away on the entity's own side.
- `GateSuffocationGuard` (new listener): suffocation damage while the entity's box overlaps a gate block → moved
  out, damage cancelled (config `gates.safety.door-suffocation-damage`, default false); players are also checked one
  tick after join and after any teleport.
- Not touched: `GateFireSystem`, `HealthSystem`, `GateDamageSink`, `GateDamageConsequenceListener` (KNG-34), gate
  tab completion (KNG-107), gate world handling (KNG-113).

## Decisions for review

1. **No suffocation damage from gate door blocks** by default (`gates.safety.door-suffocation-damage: false`); the
   entity is moved out in the same tick either way. Reversible via config.
2. `/gatedoor tp` radius 4 blocks; evacuation radius 6 blocks (to clear a lowering drawbridge's landing area).
3. The spot search does not need a walkable path from the door: with a thin wall in front, the spot may be on the
   wall's far side (still within 4 blocks, on solid ground).
4. The spawn point is used as stored (like `/warp`), without a safety search around it.
5. Entities are moved by teleport (with their riders) rather than pushed; the push is only a fallback. A minecart
   on rails is moved off the rails if the nearest safe spot has none.
