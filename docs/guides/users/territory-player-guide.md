# Territory — Player Guide

**Status:** Current · **Last updated:** 2026-11-19 (first version, territory V1 #689, docs PandiO/knk-workspace#806)
**Checked against:** knk-plugin `next` `55804b11` (not released; you see nothing of this until staff switch territory
on, and it has not been tried on a live server yet)
**Related:** [player handbook](player-handbook.md), [territory admin guide](../admin/territory-admin-guide.md) (how
staff set it up)

The land around every town belongs to that town, as far as the town's reach goes. Some land belongs to a kingdom
directly (**crown land**), some is claimed by two towns at once (**contested land**), and the rest is **wilderness**.
When you walk from one into another, a short line above your hotbar tells you where you are.

## 1. Who it is for

Everyone. There is nothing to type, nothing to pay and no cooldown: the line just appears as you walk, ride, fly or
teleport. It works the same whether you belong to a town or not.

## 2. The lines you see

The line is shown in the action bar (just above the hotbar) when you cross into a different area:

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| "Entering the territory of **Phoenix**" | You stepped onto land the town Phoenix holds |
| "Entering the crown land of **Aurelia**" | Land the kingdom Aurelia holds directly, such as a royal forest. Crown land beats any town's claim |
| "Entering contested land: **Phoenix / Rivertown**" (orange) | Two towns both reach this land and staff have not settled it yet |
| "Leaving **Phoenix**: wilderness" | You left Phoenix's land; nobody holds the land you are on now (after crown land it names the kingdom, after contested land it says "Leaving contested land: wilderness") |

1. Walking from one town's land straight into another's shows the new town's line; there is no wilderness line in
   between.
2. The line appears once per crossing, not while you walk around inside an area. Logging in shows no line.
3. A town's land reaches a set distance from the edge of each of its districts, so borders follow the town's shape
   and can run at an angle. A town's main (capital) district reaches further.
4. Staff can change the wording, or switch the lines off on a server; then you see nothing.

## 3. What it changes for you

Nothing yet besides the line: you can go, build and fight in each area exactly as before, and the protection of towns
and buildings is the same. Territory is the ground work for later features (where towns may place farms, warehouses
and shops). Other players see the same lines when they cross the same borders; nobody is told that you crossed.

## 4. If something looks wrong

1. **No lines at all:** territory is off on this server, or staff switched the lines off. Nothing is wrong with your
   account.
2. **A line names the wrong town, or contested land where you didn't expect it:** tell a staff member where you
   stood. Staff can check any spot and settle contested land.
